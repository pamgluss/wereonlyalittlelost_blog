This is a personal travel blog. It has no accounts, no comments, and no newsletter, so it never asks you for personal information.

## Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When ads are shown, Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

- You can turn off personalised advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) explains what is collected and why.
- Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and some photos are served from Cloudflare. Like any web host, both receive your IP address when your browser requests a page or image. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which posts are read. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, and your browser and device type. It does not tell me who you are.

- [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
